[docs] A guide to using Astryx globally: `astryx docs cli/global` explains why to install the CLI once for your computer, what it gives you and your agents, and how it works step by step, with what you will see. Its pages cover installing (`cli/global/install-once`), which release answers and why (`cli/global/who-answers`), updates and notices (`cli/global/updates`), the agent guide for your computer (`cli/global/computer-agent-guide`), and integrations installed for your computer (`cli/global/computer-integrations`). Integration authors get `cli/integrations/global-integrations`. Every command whose behavior outside a project differs, and every integration guide about manifests and what they add, carries an "In global mode" note linking the guide. The agent guide for your computer and `astryx doctor` outside a project point to it.
